Privacy Policy

MiRo BINGO — Game & Card

Available browser game · Effective / last updated: 13 November 2026

This notice covers the MiRo BINGO Caller and Card web applications, including Offline/Manual play, optional BINGO Sync through Cloudflare Workers, and private Linux-hosted Sync sessions.

1. Developer and contact

Developer / contact: Mark Eumir Turingan (MiRo Digital Tools). Privacy questions and requests: mygmail@markeumir.xyz. This notice concerns app functionality operated or supplied by MiRo; separately run LAN hosts and third-party platforms may control their own processing.

2. What information the app uses

  • Offline/Manual mode can use browser storage for settings, boards, or game state. Your browser keeps these on the device unless you choose an online feature.
  • In Sync mode, room identifiers, player nicknames, assigned cards, called numbers, claims, and session/game state may pass between your device and the selected game host. The service necessarily handles connection metadata such as IP addresses and request times.
  • Avoid using real names or entering other sensitive information in publicly shared room codes or nicknames.

3. Purpose and lawful basis

Information described above is used to provide the features you choose, remember local preferences, run a requested game session or device workflow, prevent duplicate or inconsistent operations, and respond to support messages. Where EU data-protection law applies to personal information under MiRo's control, the relevant basis may include the legitimate interest in delivering and securing these requested functions (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) or consent where separately required. This does not authorize unrelated advertising or marketing uses.

4. Network services and sharing

  • Offline/Manual game state is not intentionally uploaded to a MiRo game server. Optional Sync sends game traffic to Cloudflare Workers or to a privately operated Linux host, depending on the chosen mode.
  • Cloudflare may process network and diagnostic information to deliver web pages, tunnels, and Worker requests. Private room hosts can receive player and game information needed to run the session. Players may see nicknames, cards, and results necessary for play.
  • The pages and links may be hosted by third parties, which apply their own network logging and privacy practices.

Links to external platforms (such as GitHub Pages, Google Drive, MediaFire, Cloudflare and a device manufacturer's services) are subject to those services' own policies. Web hosting providers may receive routine technical request information, such as IP address and browser details. No advertising data sale is described for these MiRo app functions.

5. Retention

  • Browser data stays until you clear the site's storage or use an available reset option. Room/session data held on a Sync host is controlled by that host and may remain until expiry, administrator cleanup, or backup rotation; an exact uniform retention period cannot be promised across independently operated hosts.
  • For a session on another person's Linux server, contact that host about removal. For a MiRo-operated Sync service, contact the developer using the address below.

6. How to delete your data

  • For Offline/Manual mode, use the browser's site-data controls for the Caller and Card domains; clearing site storage can remove settings and locally stored boards.
  • For Sync sessions, leave the game and ask the hosting operator to remove any retained room/session record. Deleting browser data alone does not necessarily delete host-side records.

7. Security and permissions

  • Room codes and QR links are joining mechanisms, not a substitute for safeguarding sensitive data. Restrict links/PINs to intended participants.
  • Cloudflare-backed play uses internet infrastructure, while private-host and local modes depend on the security settings of the particular host/network.

8. Your rights and requests

Where applicable under EU/UK privacy law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection concerning personal data for which MiRo is responsible. Contact mygmail@markeumir.xyz. Some rights have legal limits. If another person operates your LAN host, direct host-side requests to that operator. You can also contact your competent data-protection supervisory authority.

9. Children and other users

These apps are not designed to ask for children's real names, addresses, or other sensitive details as a condition of gameplay. Adults supervising family play should choose non-identifying nicknames and avoid sharing screenshots or room links publicly.

10. Changes to this policy

This notice reflects the version and features identified above. Any future online, multiplayer, cloud, analytics or permission changes that affect data use will require a policy update. Review the update date at the top for the latest published notice.
