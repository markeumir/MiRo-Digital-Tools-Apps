Privacy Policy

MiRo TV Edition

Android TV / Google TV v2.0 Build 34 · Effective / last updated: 13 November 2026

This notice covers the MiRo TV Edition launcher and TV-customization features for supported Android TV / Google TV devices; it does not cover MiRoTV Restore Kit.

1. Developer and contact

Developer / contact: Mark Eumir Turingan (MiRo Digital Tools). Privacy questions and requests: mygmail@markeumir.xyz. This notice concerns app functionality operated or supplied by MiRo; separately run LAN hosts and third-party platforms may control their own processing.

2. What information the app uses

  • The application may access lists and labels of installed TV applications, selected launcher tiles, configuration values, wallpapers, photos, custom audio selections, and screensaver settings to show and personalize the TV interface.
  • Photo and audio selections can involve Android file-picker or storage permissions. The chosen media is used for user-selected local TV functions.
  • Some compatibility, device-health, or repair functions may read device model, operating-system version, package state, or other technical diagnostics available with the permissions granted.

3. Purpose and lawful basis

Information described above is used to provide the features you choose, remember local preferences, run a requested game session or device workflow, prevent duplicate or inconsistent operations, and respond to support messages. Where EU data-protection law applies to personal information under MiRo's control, the relevant basis may include the legitimate interest in delivering and securing these requested functions (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) or consent where separately required. This does not authorize unrelated advertising or marketing uses.

4. Network services and sharing

  • The described launcher and media features are intended for local device use, not for uploading personal photos or launcher configuration to a MiRo-controlled account.
  • Launching other apps, choosing external content, or using services on the TV may disclose information under those providers' separate policies. Android/Google TV system services independently process device and network data.
  • The current release should be checked for any integrated third-party libraries or telemetry before making a categorical no-data-collection claim.

Links to external platforms (such as GitHub Pages, Google Drive, MediaFire, Cloudflare and a device manufacturer's services) are subject to those services' own policies. Web hosting providers may receive routine technical request information, such as IP address and browser details. No advertising data sale is described for these MiRo app functions.

5. Retention

  • Launcher preferences and imported or referenced media can remain in app-private storage or in their original device folders until removed, application data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled.
  • Removing the launcher does not automatically erase a photo or audio file that resides in another folder or third-party app.

6. How to delete your data

  • Use the app's settings to remove custom photos/audio and reset personalization where supported. To erase app-owned local preferences, clear the app's storage in Android TV Settings, after backing up anything you want to keep.
  • Files originally selected from shared TV storage must be deleted separately using an appropriate file manager if you wish to remove the original files.

7. Security and permissions

  • Only grant storage, accessibility, or debugging-related permissions that a feature actually needs. Device-system changes are separate from data deletion.
  • Back up important settings before replacing launchers or clearing app storage; this can reset your personalized experience.

8. Your rights and requests

Where applicable under EU/UK privacy law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection concerning personal data for which MiRo is responsible. Contact mygmail@markeumir.xyz. Some rights have legal limits. If another person operates your LAN host, direct host-side requests to that operator. You can also contact your competent data-protection supervisory authority.

9. Children and other users

These apps are not designed to ask for children's real names, addresses, or other sensitive details as a condition of gameplay. Adults supervising family play should choose non-identifying nicknames and avoid sharing screenshots or room links publicly.

10. Changes to this policy

This notice reflects the version and features identified above. Any future online, multiplayer, cloud, analytics or permission changes that affect data use will require a policy update. Review the update date at the top for the latest published notice.
